Questions about blackout curtains

Do blackout curtains make a room completely dark?

The cloth can stop the light, but light still gets around a curtain: over the top of the pole, down the sides and through the gap where a pair meets. Mounting the curtain higher and wider, adding returns, or combining it with a blackout roller blind closes the gaps.

Is dimout the same as blackout?

No. Dimout fabric stops most light, often 90–95%, and leaves a room dim. Blackout fabric lets effectively no light through the cloth itself.

Can I have blackout curtains in a cloth that is not blackout?

Yes. Almost any drape can be given a blackout lining, which keeps the look of the cloth you like and adds the darkness behind it.

Are blackout curtains good for a nursery or a bedroom facing a streetlight?

That is what they are for. For the darkest result, fit a blackout roller blind inside the reveal and a blackout curtain over it, mounted higher and wider than the window.
