Curtainia is a made-to-measure curtain atelier. Tell us about the room and we design, cut and sew the drapes to fit it.

Book a design consultation Try the drape studio

Every window is different, so every pair is drawn from scratch.

We work with homeowners, interior designers and hospitality projects. You can arrive with exact measurements, a few photos, or only an idea of how the room should feel.

Together we settle the fabric, the heading and the fullness. Most pairs hang best at two to two and a half times the width of the track, and we tell you when a window wants less or more.

Each commission is cut and sewn in our workshop in Bursa, Türkiye, and sent to your door.

Four ways to dress a window

The fabric decides how a room feels at noon and at night. These are the families we make most often.

Drapery

Lined, full-bodied fabrics that hang in deep folds and close a room around you.

Sheers

Voile and semi-sheer weaves that soften daylight and keep the view.

Blackout

Double blackout cloth for bedrooms and media rooms where the dark matters.

Custom print

Your own pattern or artwork, printed to the width and drop of your window.

From first call to hanging

  1. Brief

    Tell us about the room. Photos and window sizes are enough to begin.

  2. Design

    We propose the fabric, heading and fullness, and send swatches to see and touch.

  3. Make

    Your pair is cut and sewn to your measurements in our workshop.

  4. Hang

    It ships to your door with simple guidance for hanging it well.
