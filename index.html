THE BENCH

What you are paying for, in order

Four steps that a ready-made curtain skips, and the reason yours hangs differently.

Cut

Laid flat on a four-metre table and cut to your drop, with the pattern squared to the floor rather than to the selvedge.

Weighted

Chain weight into the hem so the cloth falls straight instead of kicking out at the bottom.

Headed

Pleats set by hand to your fullness, eyelets punched here so the ring matches the ironmongery already in the room.

Hung

Every pair hangs at full drop for a day before it is folded. Cloth settles, and we would rather it settled here than in your living room.

Each cloth is woven across its own card — the same ground, the whole range of shades. We post it free, anywhere we ship. A screen will lie to you about colour and we would rather not find that out after cutting.
