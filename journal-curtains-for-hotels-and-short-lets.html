Curtains for hotels, short lets and rental projects: what to specify

A guest room curtain has a harder life than a domestic one. The decisions that matter for hospitality and rental projects, and what to put in the schedule.

In a guest room, curtains are opened and closed by people who do not own them, cleaned on a schedule, and judged at a glance by the next guest. That changes what is worth specifying.

What guests notice

  • Darkness. Light leaking around a bedroom curtain is one of the commonest complaints in reviews. Specify a blackout lining or blackout cloth, a track fitted close to the ceiling, an overlap at the centre and returns at the sides.
  • Ease of use. A curtain that sticks gets pulled, and a pulled curtain gets damaged. A good track with a wand or cord matters more than the fabric.
  • Length. A hem hovering above the floor reads as cheap. Floor length, just clear, looks right and survives vacuuming.

What operators should ask for

  • Two layers. A sheer for daytime privacy and a lined drape for the night, on a double track.
  • Cloth that cleans. Ask how each fabric is to be cleaned and how it behaves afterwards, before choosing it for forty rooms.
  • Fire performance. Requirements depend on the country, the building and its use. Ask your fire consultant or local authority what standard applies, then ask the supplier for test evidence for the specific cloth. Do not accept a general assurance.
  • Repeatability. You will need replacements. Make sure the supplier keeps your schedule and can make one more curtain in the same cloth later.

The schedule

A spreadsheet is enough: room, window, track length, drop, layer (sheer or drape), cloth, heading, lining. With that, a workshop can price every window individually and deliver floor by floor.

Short answers

What curtains are best for a short-let or Airbnb bedroom?

A sheer for daytime privacy plus a blackout-lined drape on a double track, floor length, fitted high and wide so light does not leak around the edges.

Do hotel curtains need to be fire retardant?

Requirements vary by country, building and use. Confirm the applicable standard with a fire consultant or local authority and ask the supplier for test evidence for the specific fabric.
