Blackout, dimout and room darkening: what the words actually mean

Three terms, used inconsistently. What each one does to the light, why no curtain makes a room pitch dark on its own, and how to get a properly dark bedroom.

These three words describe how much light a cloth stops. They are not regulated terms, so it helps to know what a seller means by them.

The three levels

  • Blackout. The cloth itself lets effectively no light through, usually because of a coating or a dense inner layer. Hold it to a lamp and you see nothing.
  • Dimout. A tightly woven cloth that stops most light, often quoted as a percentage such as 90% or 95%, without a coating. Softer to handle and drapes more like an ordinary fabric. In daylight the room is dim, not dark.
  • Room darkening. A loose term for anything that makes a room noticeably darker. It can mean dimout, or simply a heavy lined curtain.

Why the room still is not dark

Even with a true blackout cloth, light gets around the curtain: over the top of the pole, down the sides, and through the gap where a pair meets. In a bright street this halo is often brighter than people expect. It is not a fault in the fabric.

How to get a dark room

  • Mount the track or pole higher and wider than the window, so the curtain overlaps the wall on all sides.
  • Use a track that sits close to the wall or ceiling; a pole on brackets leaves a larger gap at the top.
  • Let the pair overlap in the middle, and add returns so the outer edges turn back to the wall.
  • For the darkest result, fit a blackout roller blind inside the reveal and a curtain over it.

Lining as an alternative

You do not have to choose a blackout face fabric. Almost any drape can be given a blackout lining, which keeps the look of the cloth you like and adds the darkness behind it. Tell us how dark the room needs to be and we will suggest the combination; the light control guide covers the options.

Short answers

Is dimout the same as blackout?

No. Dimout fabric stops most light, often 90–95%, and leaves a room dim. Blackout fabric lets effectively no light through the cloth itself.

Do blackout curtains make a room completely dark?

Not alone. Light leaks around the top, sides and centre. Mounting the curtain higher and wider, adding returns, or combining it with a blackout blind closes the gaps.
