At 100 g/m² this sits in the classic voile range: it softens the light and blurs the view in without darkening the room.

Jok is woven 300 cm (118 in) wide. We turn the cloth on its side, so the width of the roll becomes the drop of your curtain: any length up to 280 cm (110 in) is cut in one piece, and the curtain can be as wide as you like with no vertical seam.

For the top of the curtain we would suggest ripple fold for an even wave, double pinch pleat for a tailored look, or a flat panel when the sheer should read as a single plane. The bench lists all eight headings and the fullness we use for each.

Questions about Jok

How wide is Jok and will my curtain have a seam?

Jok is woven 300 cm (118 in) wide. We turn the cloth on its side, so the width of the roll becomes the drop of your curtain: any length up to 280 cm (110 in) is cut in one piece, and the curtain can be as wide as you like with no vertical seam.

Can I get a sample of Jok?

Yes. Samples are free, including the postage, anywhere we ship. Write to info@curtainia.com with the cloth name and your address.

Is there a minimum order for Jok?

No. One window is an order, for private and trade customers alike.

Do you ship Jok curtains to the United States?

Yes — delivered duty paid, so the import duty is inside the quoted price. We also export to the EU, the UK and worldwide, quoted delivered per country.

How much do Jok curtains cost?

$48.49 per metre of cloth used, delivered duty paid to the United States — the making, the import duty and the freight are inside that figure. One panel on a 1 m track is about $107; a pair for an 8 ft window is about $256. There is no minimum order.
