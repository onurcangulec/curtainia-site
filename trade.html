Trade & projects

For the people who specify, not just the people who live there.

Interior designers, architects, hotels, short-let operators, furniture makers and shopfitters. The making is the same; what changes is how we work with you — pricing from the first window, a sample box that stays on your shelf, and one person who answers.

No minimum order

One window or a whole floor. We do not price a single curtain as a favour and a hundred as a contract — the per-window figure is the same.

A sample box you keep

Hangers of the cloths you specify most, sent once and restocked when a range changes. Yours to put in front of a client.

White label if you need it

Plain packaging, no Curtainia labels in the box, no paperwork with our name on it inside the parcel. Say the word on the first order.

Quotes in writing, in a day

A schedule of windows in, a priced schedule back. Nothing verbal, nothing you have to chase.

Landed pricing

Duty-paid figures for the US, the UK and the EU, so your client sees one number and your margin is not eaten by a customs bill.

Repeat orders held

We keep your measurements, headings and cloths on file. A second phase is a reference, not a re-measure.

Straight answer

We will tell you when we are not the right supplier.

If the deadline is too tight for a made-to-measure pair, or the budget is in ready-made territory, we would rather say so on the first email than take the order and disappoint you on site. A supplier who says no occasionally is worth more than one who says yes every time.
