How to measure for a roller blind: inside or outside the recess

A roller blind is cut to the millimetre, so the measurement has to be right. The two ways to fit one, which numbers to take, and the mistakes that cause most remakes.

A curtain forgives a centimetre. A roller blind does not: too wide and it will not go in, too narrow and light comes down both sides. Take the measurement with a steel tape and write it down in millimetres.

First decide where it goes

  • Inside the recess (inside mount). The blind sits within the window opening. Neat, takes up no wall, leaves the sill usable. A small gap either side of the cloth is unavoidable, because the brackets and mechanism are wider than the fabric.
  • Outside the recess (outside mount). The blind is fixed to the wall or frame above the opening and overlaps it. Blocks more light and hides an uneven opening. The better choice when darkness matters.

Measuring inside the recess

  1. Width: measure across the opening at the top, the middle and the bottom. Give us the smallest figure. Walls are rarely parallel.
  2. Drop: measure from the top of the recess to the sill on the left, in the centre and on the right. Give us the largest figure.
  3. Depth: check there is room for the bracket and roll, and that handles or tiles will not block the cloth.

Tell us these are recess sizes. We make the allowance for the brackets; do not deduct it yourself, or it will be taken off twice.

Measuring outside the recess

  1. Width: the opening plus the overlap you want. Around 5–10 cm (2–4 in) each side is usual; more for a bedroom.
  2. Drop: from where the brackets will sit, typically 10–15 cm above the opening, to below the sill.

Two decisions to make

  • Chain side. Left or right. Think about where the sink, door or furniture is.
  • Roll direction. Standard roll drops the cloth close to the glass. Reverse roll brings it forward to clear handles.

Blackout or panama, both are cut to your figures; the blinds page explains the two cloths. If in doubt, send the numbers and a photograph and we will check them with you.

Short answers

Should a roller blind go inside or outside the recess?

Inside looks neater and saves space but leaves small light gaps at the sides. Outside overlaps the opening and blocks more light, so it is better for bedrooms.

Do I deduct anything from the recess width when ordering a blind?

No. Give the exact recess size and say it is a recess measurement. The maker deducts the bracket allowance; deducting it yourself makes the blind too narrow.
