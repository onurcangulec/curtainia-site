Cloth library

Ninety-nine cloths. Fifty-three drapes, forty-six sheers.

Every cloth here is on the shelf today. Widths run 290 to 340 cm, so most windows are cut from a single width with no seam across the face. Weights run from 25 g/m² — a net you can read a newspaper through — to 375 g/m², which hangs like a coat.

What they feel like

Eight cloths, described the way we would describe them on the phone.

OAK

A dry, open weave that reads like raw linen in daylight.

ELM

The heaviest cloth on the shelf. Hangs dead straight, 95% blackout.

CEDAR

Dense and quiet. A room-darkening drape that still looks soft.

ASH

Fine slub through a matt ground — texture you notice up close.

WALNUT

Deep tone, close weave, almost no light through it.

BEECH

Mid-weight and forgiving. The safe answer for a first pair.

ALDER

A warm neutral that sits between oat and clay.

SPRUCE

Heavy, flat, architectural. Good for very tall windows.
