Why extra-wide fabric makes a better curtain

Most curtain fabric is 54 inches wide and has to be joined to cover a window. Cloth woven 3 metres wide can be turned on its side, so the curtain is one piece with no seams.

Standard furnishing fabric is woven about 54 inches (137 cm) wide. A curtain for anything but a narrow window needs more cloth across than that, so widths are sewn together side by side. Every join is a vertical seam running from top to hem.

What seams cost you

  • On a sheer, a seam shows as a line whenever light comes through it.
  • On a patterned cloth, each seam has to be matched, which wastes fabric.
  • Seams can pucker or hang differently from the cloth around them as the curtain ages.

Turning the cloth on its side

Fabric woven around 3 metres wide solves this. Instead of hanging the cloth the way it comes off the roll, the maker turns it ninety degrees. The width of the roll becomes the drop of the curtain, and the length of the roll, which is effectively unlimited, becomes its width. Upholsterers call this railroading.

The result is a curtain made from one continuous piece. A window 20 feet wide gets a curtain with no vertical seam at all.

The one limit

The drop cannot exceed the width of the roll, less what is needed for the heading and hem. With our cloth, woven 290–360 cm wide, the longest seamless drop is 270–340 cm (106–134 in) depending on the fabric. That covers a floor-to-ceiling curtain under a 9 ft ceiling in almost the whole library, and under a 10 ft ceiling in the widest cloths. Taller than that, we tell you before we cut, and discuss a horizontal join placed where it is least visible.

What to check

If you are comparing quotes for a wide window, ask each maker how wide the fabric is and whether the curtain will have vertical seams. Every cloth page in our library lists the roll width and the longest seamless drop for that fabric.

Short answers

What is railroaded curtain fabric?

Fabric turned ninety degrees so the width of the roll runs top to bottom. With wide-width cloth this lets a curtain of any width be made in one piece with no vertical seams.

How tall can a seamless curtain be?

As tall as the fabric is wide, less the heading and hem. With 290–360 cm cloth, the longest seamless drop is about 270–340 cm (106–134 in).
