Made to measure, custom or ready-made: what are you paying for?

Ready-made curtains come in fixed sizes. Made-to-measure curtains are cut to your window. What actually differs, when ready-made is the sensible choice, and when it is not.

The three terms are used loosely, so here is what each normally means.

  • Ready-made. Sewn in advance in a few standard widths and lengths, commonly 84, 96 and 108 inch drops in the US. You pick the nearest size.
  • Made to measure (often called custom in the US). Nothing exists until you order. The curtain is cut and sewn to the width and drop you give, in the cloth, heading and fullness you choose.
  • Bespoke. Made to measure with a maker involved in the decisions: visiting or advising, proposing cloth, solving awkward windows.

What you get for the difference

  • Length that is right. A ready-made hem lands wherever the standard size puts it. A made-to-measure hem stops where you want it.
  • Enough cloth. Ready-made panels are often sold with little fullness to keep the price down. Made to measure lets you choose 2× or more.
  • The heading you want. Ready-made is mostly grommet, rod pocket or tape. Pinch pleat and ripple fold are usually made to order.
  • Choice of cloth. You are choosing from a library, not from what was produced in bulk.

When ready-made is the right answer

If the window is a standard size, the room is temporary, or the budget is tight, a ready-made pair is a perfectly good decision, and you can have it tomorrow. We would rather say that than sell you something you do not need.

When it is not

Tall ceilings, wide glazing, bay windows, a drop that falls between standard sizes, a room that needs proper darkness, or simply a room you intend to keep. Those are the cases where a curtain cut for the window earns its price.

Short answers

Are made-to-measure curtains worth it?

For non-standard windows, tall ceilings, or rooms where length, fullness and darkness matter, yes. For standard windows on a tight budget, ready-made is a sensible choice.

Is custom the same as made to measure?

In practice, yes. 'Custom' is the usual American term and 'made to measure' the British one for curtains cut and sewn to the buyer's own dimensions.
