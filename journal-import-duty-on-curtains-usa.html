Do you pay import duty on curtains shipped to the USA?

Yes, someone does. The question is whether it is inside the price you were quoted or waiting for you at the door. How duty on imported curtains works, and what delivered duty paid changes.

Curtains made abroad and sent to an address in the United States are an import, and imports of textiles carry duty. The amount depends on what the curtain is made of and where it was made, and the rules have been changing often enough that any figure printed here would age badly. What does not change is the mechanism, and the mechanism is what decides whether your order is pleasant or not.

Who pays, and when

There are two ways a seller can ship to you.

  • Duties unpaid (often written DAP, DDU or simply "plus duties and taxes"). The price you pay the seller covers the goods and perhaps the freight. The courier pays the duty at the border on your behalf, then bills you, usually with a handling fee, before or at delivery. You find out the amount when the bill arrives.
  • Delivered duty paid (DDP). The seller works out the duty in advance, includes it in the price, and settles it before the parcel reaches you. Nothing is collected at your door.

The old allowance that let low-value parcels into the US free of duty has been suspended, so at the time of writing even a small order is assessed. That makes the difference between the two methods matter for every order, not only the large ones.

What to ask before you order from abroad

  • Is the quote delivered duty paid, in writing?
  • Is freight included, and is it tracked door to door?
  • Who is the importer of record? If the answer is you, expect paperwork.
  • What happens if the courier still asks you for money?

Short answers

Is there import duty on curtains sent to the USA?

Yes. Imported curtains are assessed for duty. With delivered duty paid (DDP) shipping the seller includes and pays it; otherwise the courier bills the buyer before or at delivery.

What does DDP mean for a curtain order?

Delivered duty paid: the quoted price already contains the import duty and customs formalities, so nothing is collected from the buyer on delivery.
