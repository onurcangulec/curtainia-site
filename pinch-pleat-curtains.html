Custom pinch pleat curtains, double or triple.

Groups of folds pinched together and stitched at the base, with flat spaces between them. The pleats are fixed, so the curtain falls in the same controlled folds every time you close it. A double pinch is the everyday choice — tidy, not fussy. A triple uses more cloth, throws a deeper shadow and dresses the window.

Fullness
2.0× for a double pinch, 2.5× for a triple. On a 100-inch track a double takes 200 inches of cloth.
Hangs on
A pole with rings or a track with hooks. It is forgiving about hardware.
Suits
Heavier drapes, lined and blackout curtains, traditional or tailored rooms. In a heavy sheer, a double pinch looks tailored.
Made
Each pleat formed and stitched by hand, weighted hem, pressed, then measured again against your figures before it is boxed.

Questions about pinch pleat curtains

What is the difference between double and triple pinch pleat?

A double has two folds per pleat and is the everyday choice. A triple has three, uses more cloth — 2.5× fullness against 2.0× — and looks dressier.

Can pinch pleat curtains go on a curtain rod?

Yes. Pinch pleats hang on a pole with rings or on a track with hooks.

How much cloth does a curtain take?

Fullness is the ratio of cloth to track. At 2.0× a 100-inch track takes 200 inches of fabric. Under about 1.5× a curtain looks thin when it is closed, however good the cloth is.

Is there a minimum order?

No. One window is an order, for private and trade customers alike.

Can I get samples first?

Yes. Samples are free, including the postage, anywhere we ship. Write to info@curtainia.com with the cloth names and your address.

Do you ship pinch pleat curtains to the United States and the UK?

Yes. To the USA we ship delivered duty paid, so the import duty is inside the quoted price. We also export to the UK, the EU and worldwide, quoted delivered per country.
