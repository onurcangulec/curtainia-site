How long should curtains be for 8, 9 and 10 ft ceilings?

Where to mount the pole, where the hem should stop, and the drop that follows from it for the most common ceiling heights, in inches and centimetres.

Curtain length is not the height of the window. It is the distance from where the curtain hangs to where it stops, and both of those are choices.

Where the top goes

Mounting the pole or track well above the window frame makes the window, and the room, look taller. A common rule is to go at least 4–6 inches (10–15 cm) above the frame, or about halfway between the frame and the ceiling. With a ceiling-fixed track the curtain simply starts at the ceiling.

Where the hem stops

  • Floor length, just clear. The hem stops about ½ inch (1–2 cm) above the floor. Clean, practical, and the right answer for curtains you open and close every day.
  • Sill length. The hem stops at or just below the sill. Used above radiators, worktops and window seats.
  • Puddled. An extra inch or more rests on the floor. It looks soft in photographs and collects dust in real life.

Typical drops

These assume a floor-length curtain that just clears the floor. Your own figure depends on your hardware, so measure before ordering.

  • 8 ft ceiling (244 cm): pole a few inches below the ceiling gives a drop of roughly 88–92 in (224–234 cm); a ceiling track gives about 95 in (241 cm).
  • 9 ft ceiling (274 cm): roughly 100–104 in (254–264 cm) from a pole, about 107 in (272 cm) from a ceiling track.
  • 10 ft ceiling (305 cm): roughly 112–116 in (284–295 cm) from a pole, about 119 in (302 cm) from a ceiling track.

Why this matters when you buy

Ready-made curtains come in a handful of lengths, commonly 84, 96 and 108 inches, which is why so many end up hovering above the floor or bunching on it. A made-to-measure curtain is cut to your figure. Our cloth is woven 290–360 cm wide and turned on its side, so drops up to 270–340 cm (106–134 in), depending on the cloth, are made in one piece with no seam. Short answers

Should curtains touch the floor?

For curtains used daily, stop the hem about half an inch (1–2 cm) above the floor. They look floor length but do not drag or collect dust.

How high above the window should a curtain pole be?

At least 4–6 inches (10–15 cm) above the frame, or about halfway between the frame and the ceiling, to make the window look taller.
