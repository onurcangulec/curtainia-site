The mill weight for this cloth is not yet confirmed, so we would rather post you a sample than describe it from memory.

Polar is woven 305 cm (120 in) wide. We turn the cloth on its side, so the width of the roll becomes the drop of your curtain: any length up to 285 cm (112 in) is cut in one piece, and the curtain can be as wide as you like with no vertical seam.

For the top of the curtain we would suggest double pinch pleat as the everyday answer, triple pinch for a dressier window, ripple fold for a modern one, or grommet for something casual. The bench lists all eight headings and the fullness we use for each.

Questions about Polar

How wide is Polar and will my curtain have a seam?

Polar is woven 305 cm (120 in) wide. We turn the cloth on its side, so the width of the roll becomes the drop of your curtain: any length up to 285 cm (112 in) is cut in one piece, and the curtain can be as wide as you like with no vertical seam.

Can I get a sample of Polar?

Yes. Samples are free, including the postage, anywhere we ship. Write to info@curtainia.com with the cloth name and your address.

Is there a minimum order for Polar?

No. One window is an order, for private and trade customers alike.

Do you ship Polar curtains to the United States?

Yes — delivered duty paid, so the import duty is inside the quoted price. We also export to the EU, the UK and worldwide, quoted delivered per country.

How much do Polar curtains cost?

$85.17 per metre of cloth used, delivered duty paid to the United States — the making, the import duty and the freight are inside that figure. One panel on a 1 m track is about $187; a pair for an 8 ft window is about $450. There is no minimum order.
