Privacy, cookies & terms

This site is the atelier's showcase. It sells nothing, takes no payment and holds no account, so there is less to say here than you might expect. What there is, is said plainly.

Privacy

What the website itself collects

These pages are static files and carry no advertising. The only measurement is Google Analytics, and it does not run until you accept it — see Cookies below. Beyond that, nothing until you write to us.

The enquiry form

If you fill in the form on the contact page, what you type is sent to a mail script on our own server and arrives as an email at info@curtainia.com. We use no third-party form service. The message holds what you chose to put in it, plus the time and the IP address the form was sent from, which is how the script stops automated spam.

What your browser still talks to

The typefaces are served by Google Fonts and these pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Loading them means your browser contacts those services, which see your IP address and basic request information the way they do for any site that uses them. We receive none of it.

What we hold once you write to us

Your name, your email address, whatever you tell us about the project, and the correspondence that follows. We use it to answer you and to run the commission if it goes ahead. We do not sell it and we do not market to you without being asked.

If a commission proceeds we also hold what is needed to make and ship it — measurements, a delivery address, and the paperwork of an international shipment. Shipping and customs partners receive only what they need in order to deliver.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that go nowhere are cleared out once they are plainly dead. Records tied to an actual order are kept for as long as commercial and tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to info@curtainia.com and we will deal with it.

Cookies

This site uses one thing that can set cookies: Google Analytics 4, which tells us how many people visit and which pages help. Nothing else — no advertising pixel, no social tracker, no session store.

It does not run unless you let it. The first time you arrive, a strip at the bottom asks. Until you press Accept, no Analytics script loads and not a single request reaches Google. Press Decline and nothing loads, now or later; anything an earlier choice left in your browser is cleared on the spot. We use Google Consent Mode, with every storage permission denied by default.

If you accept, Google Analytics sets cookies named _ga and _ga_<id> to tell one visit from another. IP addresses are anonymised. We see totals and trends, never an individual. You can change your mind at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser, which brings the strip back.

Your own accept-or-decline choice is kept in your browser’s local storage, which is not a cookie and is never sent to us.

Terms of use

What this site is

These pages describe what the atelier makes and how it works. They are not an offer to sell, and nothing here forms a contract. Any commission is agreed separately in writing, with its own specification, price and delivery terms.

Colour on a screen

Every photograph on this site was taken or rendered under one light, and your display is not ours. We confirm every shade against a physical sample before anything is cut, and the sample is what counts. Samples are free — ask.

Made-to-measure work

Curtains and blinds are cut and sewn to measurements you provide. Because each piece is made for one window, small variations in colour, texture and finished size are normal in textile work and are not faults. Where a tolerance matters, we agree it with you before cutting.

Prices and duty

Quotes for the United States are given delivered duty paid: import duty and the customs formalities are inside the figure and are not collected from you on delivery. A quote is valid for the period stated on it.
