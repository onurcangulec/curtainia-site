Privacy & terms

This site is a showcase for the atelier. It sells nothing, takes no payment and keeps no account, so there is less to say here than you might expect.

Privacy

What this website collects

Nothing. These pages are static files. They set no cookies, run no analytics and carry no advertising or tracking scripts. Nothing you type into the consultation form is sent to us by the website itself — the form simply opens your own email program with the message prepared, and you decide whether to send it.

What your browser still talks to

The typefaces on these pages are served by Google Fonts, and the pages themselves are hosted on GitHub Pages. Loading them means your browser contacts those services, which will see your IP address and basic request information the way they do for any website that uses them. We receive none of it.

What we hold once you write to us

When you email us, we hold what you chose to send: your name, your email address, whatever you tell us about the project, and the correspondence that follows. We use it to answer you and to run the commission if it goes ahead. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for marketing you did not ask for.

If a commission proceeds, we also hold what is needed to make and ship it — measurements, a delivery address, and the paperwork that goes with an international shipment. Shipping and customs partners receive only what they need to deliver.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that go nowhere are cleared out once they are plainly dead. Records tied to an actual order are kept as long as commercial and tax law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to info@curtainia.com and we will deal with it.

Terms of use

What this site is

These pages describe what the atelier makes and how it works. They are not an offer to sell, and nothing here forms a contract. Any commission is agreed separately in writing, with its own specification, price and delivery terms.

The drape studio

The drape studio draws an impression of a curtain, not a specification. Colours on a screen depend on your display, and the drawing simplifies how real cloth hangs. We confirm every shade against a physical swatch before anything is cut, and the swatch is what counts.

Made-to-measure work

Curtains are cut and sewn to measurements you provide. Because each pair is made for one window, small variations in colour, texture and finished size are normal in textile work and are not faults. We agree tolerances with you before cutting where it matters.
