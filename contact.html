What helps most

A photograph

Taken from inside, with the daylight behind the window. It tells us more than any description.

The track length

Pole to pole, or the track you intend to fit — not the width of the glass.

The drop

From the track to where you want the curtain to stop: sill, below sill, or floor.

How dark

Blackout, room-darkening or just privacy. This decides the cloth more than colour does.
