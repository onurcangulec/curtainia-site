Pinch pleat or ripple fold: which heading should you choose?

Two headings account for most made-to-measure curtains. One is tailored and traditional, the other is an even modern wave. What each needs, what each costs you in cloth, and how to pick.

The heading is the top few inches of a curtain, and it decides how the whole thing hangs. Pinch pleat and ripple fold are the two that most people end up choosing between.

Pinch pleat

Groups of folds are pinched together and stitched at the base, with flat spaces between them. A double pinch has two folds per group and is the everyday choice; a triple has three, uses more cloth and looks dressier. The pleats are fixed, so the curtain falls in the same controlled folds every time you close it.

  • Hangs on: a pole with rings or a track with hooks. It is forgiving about hardware.
  • Fullness: about 2× for a double pinch, 2.5× for a triple.
  • Suits: heavier drapes, lined curtains, traditional or tailored rooms.

Ripple fold

The curtain is flat cloth with snap tape along the top, clipped to carriers that are spaced on a cord inside a dedicated track. The spacing forces the cloth into a continuous S-shaped wave that runs evenly from top to hem.

  • Hangs on: a ripple fold track only. It cannot go on an ordinary pole.
  • Fullness: about 2×, set by the carrier spacing.
  • Suits: sheers, lighter drapes, wall-to-wall and floor-to-ceiling glazing, modern rooms.

How to decide

Start with the hardware you have or want. If there is already a pole on the wall, pinch pleat is the natural answer. If you are fitting a ceiling track across a wide run of glass, ripple fold stacks back more neatly and looks calmer. Then think about weight: a heavy drape holds a pinch pleat beautifully, while a sheer shows off the even wave of a ripple fold.

Short answers

Is ripple fold more expensive than pinch pleat?

The cloth needed is similar, around 2× fullness for both. Ripple fold needs its own track system, which is usually the larger cost difference.

Can ripple fold curtains hang on a normal curtain pole?

No. Ripple fold relies on carriers spaced inside a dedicated track. On a pole, choose pinch pleat, grommet or tab top instead.
