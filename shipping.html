Why it matters

DDP is not a shipping option. It is a promise about the final price.

Delivered duty paid is an Incoterm: the seller carries the cost and the risk until the goods reach you, import charges included. The alternative you will usually be offered — delivered at place, or simply "plus duties" — moves that bill onto you, at a number nobody will name up front.

We are a small atelier. We would rather lose a quote on an honest total than win one on a number that grows.

Questions people actually ask

The six that come up every week.

Will I be asked to pay duty when it arrives?

No. Delivered duty paid means the import duty and the formalities are settled before the parcel reaches you. The figure in your quote is the figure you pay.

Who is the importer of record?

Curtainia Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales under number 17203286. You are the buyer, not the importer.

How long does it take?

Making takes the bulk of it. Once the pair leaves the bench, a tracked door-to-door service is the normal route. We give you a date in writing with the quote and tell you the day it ships.

What if the measurement was wrong?

Write to us before you hang them. A made-to-measure pair cannot be returned to a shelf, but a hem can usually be let down or taken up, and we would rather fix it than argue about it.

Do you ship outside the United States?

Yes — the UK, the EU and most of the world. The duty-paid arrangement is quoted per country, because the rules differ.

Are samples really free?

Yes, including the postage, anywhere we ship. We would rather send you five cloths than sell you the wrong one.
